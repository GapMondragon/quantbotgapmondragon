STAGE 03 / 07 // 6-PHASE ALPHA PIPELINE ACTIVE PHASE 01 / 06 — IDEA GENERATION
Strategy Development Process

Empowering Investors with Quantitative Expertise

From initial mathematical intuition to round-the-clock live electronic execution: scroll through our structured 6-phase pipeline to see how each stage eliminates emotion and ensures repeatable alpha.

PHASE 01 // HYPOTHESIS FORMULATION GATE CHECK: Alpha Intuition & Specialist Research Alignment
01

Idea Generation

Start with a basic strategy idea, such as in futures or cash equities, and collaborate directly with our specialist data and research teams.

02

Data Analysis

Gain access to necessary datasets, partition into in-sample and out-of-sample datasets, and thoroughly analyze data quality and market outliers.

03

Code Development

Test the hypothesis on in-sample data using our development environment (Python, C++, R), evaluating performance against rigorous empirical benchmarks.

04

Trading Logic & Risk

If in-sample results meet expectations, further develop the strategy by implementing trading logic and risk management using our proprietary backtesting platform.

05

Paper Trading & Testing

Deploy the strategy in paper trading mode, monitoring daily performance and comparing with previous backtests to guarantee execution accuracy and remove lookahead bias.

06

Live Trading

Once all benchmarks, risk tolerances, and execution hurdles are met, the strategy is fully deployed into actual live production trading.
