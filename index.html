STRATEGY DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Empowering Investors with Quantitative Expertise and Cutting-Edge Solutions

01

Idea Generation

Start with a basic strategy idea, such as in futures or cash equities, and collaborate directly with our research and data team.

02

Data Analysis

Gain access to necessary datasets, partition into in-sample and out-of-sample data, and analyze quality, integrity, and market outliers.

03

Code Development

Test the hypothesis on in-sample data using our development environment (Python, C++, R), evaluating its performance against benchmarks.

04

Trading Logic & Risk

If in-sample results meet expectations, further develop the strategy by implementing trading logic and risk management using our backtesting platform.

05

Paper Trading & Testing

Deploy the strategy in paper trading mode, monitoring daily performance and comparing it with previous backtest results to eliminate lookahead bias.

06

Live Trading

Once all empirical benchmarks, risk tolerances, and execution criteria are met, the strategy is ready for actual live production trading.
