02 / RESEARCH LIFECYCLE

Empirical Rigor. Six Deterministic Stages.

Every quantitative strategy deployed at Quantbot follows a mandatory six-stage progression from initial hypothesis to continuous live production.

STAGE 01 ACTIVE HYPOTHESIS

Idea Generation

Systematic hypothesis modeling across global cash equities and electronic futures. Researchers collaborate directly with our internal data engineering desk to isolate tradeable anomalies.

UNIVERSE: GLOBAL EQUITIES & FUTURES
STAGE 02 DATA HYGIENE

Data Analysis

Curated acquisition of tick-level and alternative market feeds. Datasets are strictly partitioned into in-sample (60%) and out-of-sample sets to eliminate look-ahead bias and audit microstructure outliers.

PARTITIONING: IN-SAMPLE · OUT-OF-SAMPLE
STAGE 03 LINUX HPC

Code Development

Strategy formulation using C++, Python, and R on high-performance Linux compute clusters. Empirical backtesting rigorously validates performance against historical baseline benchmarks.

LANGUAGES: C++ · PYTHON · R
STAGE 04 FACTOR RISK

Trading Logic & Risk

Integration of proprietary Transaction Cost Models (TCM) and multi-factor risk overlays. Enforces hard portfolio bounds, liquidity constraints, and drawdown limiters before capital allocation.

RISK CONTROL: TCM · FACTOR MODELS · LIMITS
STAGE 05 SIMULATION

Paper Testing

Simulated production deployment against real-time exchange order books. Daily operations track realized slippage, fill rates, and execution drag against modeled backtests.

AUDIT: DAILY RECONCILIATION · FILLS
STAGE 06 24/7 EXECUTION

Production Execution

Deployment into live institutional trading. Strategies run autonomously across co-located exchange gateways in North America, Europe, and Asia with 24/7 technical monitoring.

DEPLOYMENT: 24/7 AUTOMATED LIVE DESK
