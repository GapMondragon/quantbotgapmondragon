Privacy Notice

Our commitment to protecting non-public personal information.

This notice describes the privacy policy adopted by Quantbot Technologies LP ("Q") to protect the non-public personal information ("Nonpublic Information") of persons who are or may become its clients.

Scope of Nonpublic Information

Nonpublic Information may include, but is not limited to your name, address, telephone number, social security number or tax identification number, personal financial data such as net worth or income, bank or brokerage account information or delivery instructions. Q may gain access to Nonpublic Information directly from you via telephone, e-mail or fax, or through documents provided to us by yourself or a third party.

Information Sharing Practices

Q will share Nonpublic Information with its affiliated companies to assist in providing the services that Q has been requested to perform for you.

It is Q’s policy not to share Nonpublic Information with any non-affiliated third party except to assist in providing the services that Q has been requested to perform for you, or to the extent reasonably necessary to prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions, or as required by regulatory authorities or law enforcement.

Confidentiality and Safeguards

Q employees who have been granted access to Nonpublic Information are bound by the firm’s privacy policy to maintain and protect its confidentiality. Other parties who have been granted access to Nonpublic Information by Q are similarly bound by nondisclosure agreements.

Q protects Nonpublic Information by maintaining physical, electronic and procedural safeguards. If Q shares Nonpublic Information with a nonaffiliated entity under circumstances not covered by a regulatory exception, the firm will deliver to each affected client an opportunity to opt out of such information sharing.

Questions & Compliance Contact

If you have questions regarding Quantbot Technologies LP’s Privacy Policy, please email debra.wisdom@quantbot.com or call (646) 461-6204.

Pursuant to the Investment Advisors Act of 1940, please note that Quantbot Technologies LP’s Form ADV is available to view online at http://brokercheck.finra.org/ or directly from Quantbot Technologies LP upon written request.
