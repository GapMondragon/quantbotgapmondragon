Proof of Concept (POC) Program

Proof Of Concept

At Quantbot, we offer a unique POC program designed for research professionals in the field of quantitative finance.

Our POC setup provides an opportunity for experienced individuals to test and validate their investment ideas using a realistic simulation infrastructure, with full cost coverage and dedicated quantitative guidance.

Idea Validation

Exploring and Verifying Investment Ideas

The POC program provides a structured, risk-free environment to turn mathematical hypotheses into validated trading strategies.

Clear Advancement Path

Direct path to advancement into other Quantbot programs, such as becoming a Portfolio Manager within QMM or joining our core quantitative research team.

Ideal for Talented Researchers

Tailored specifically for researchers and academics with exceptional quantitative insight who may not yet have a formal track record in live production trading.

Realistic Simulation Platform

Utilizes our institutional-grade simulation infrastructure for thorough proposal exploration, accurate backtesting, and outlier verification.

Institutional Data

Access to Quant Data Store

POC Consultants receive the same comprehensive data access as our permanent employees and portfolio managers.

Curated Data Mart

Our data mart offers a wide variety of clean, curated, and standardized historic datasets ready for instant analytical exploration.

Market & Alternative Feeds

Comprehensive tick and bar market data, analyst recommendations, alternative sentiment feeds, and global corporate event streams.

Risk & Cost Models

Direct access to leading multi-factor global risk models and realistic transaction cost models (TCA) for robust slippage estimates.

Full Equality of Access

No restricted 'demo tiers': POC Consultants operate with full access to internal data infrastructure, documentation, and tools.

High-Performance Compute

Flexible Computing Platform

Work in an institutional Linux development environment equipped with your preferred programming languages and HPC clusters.

Cloud-Based Computing

Linux Development Stack

Modern Linux toolchains, version control, automated batch scheduling, and continuous integration testing tools.

Language of Choice

Develop seamlessly using Python, C++, or R with standardized bindings into our simulation platform.
